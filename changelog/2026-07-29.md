# Benchmark figures aligned with pipeline stats — week of 2026-07-27

A short week focused on making benchmark visuals consistent with the statistics they report.

## Updates

- **Bootstrap CIs on reward curves.** Reward-over-time plots now show bootstrap confidence intervals instead of parametric error, matching what the analysis pipeline already reports.
- **Bootstrap error bars on violation and deadlock charts.** Violation-rate and deadlock-frequency bar charts switched from standard deviation to bootstrap CI error bars, so uncertainty bands agree across every figure in a run.
- **Pareto frontier overlay.** The strategy comparison chart now overlays a Pareto frontier, making it easier to spot strategies that trade off reward and safety without dominating.
- **Unified color palette.** All four benchmark figure types share a single palette, so a strategy keeps the same color across reward curves, bar charts, and the Pareto view. See the [experiment protocol](/book/appendix-d-experiment-protocol) for how figures are produced.
